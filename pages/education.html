African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) Rwanda

Kigali, Rwanda (2024 – 2025)
M.Sc. in Mathematical Sciences · Gates Foundation Scholar

Majored in infectious disease modelling. My thesis, supervised at the Center for Epidemiological Modeling and Analysis (CEMA), asked how case management in private drug shops, including failure to test, affects malaria treatment outcomes in Kenya. I built an extended SEIR model driven by CHIRPS rainfall and fitted it to monthly malaria incidence, with drug shop behaviour taken from a national survey of over 400 outlets. In the model, universal test-and-treat raised national recovery rates by about 16.5% and prevented more than 835,000 return visits over 2025 to 2026.
